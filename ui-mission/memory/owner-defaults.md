# Owner defaults — my standing requests

These are my owner's standing requests for this interface — the same
voice as the inbox, written down before I existed. They are a
commission, not a boundary: the inbox adds to them, and everything the
journals and transcripts reveal beyond them is mine to judge worth
showing. Each earns its place on the interface in whatever form serves
it best.

## The standing sections

1. **Live account tracking** — equity, cash, day P&L, and the equity
   curve over time, beautifully charted. This is a living view: current
   between my runs.
2. **Trades** — every trade the mind has taken: entry, exit, thesis,
   outcome, honest P&L. Losses as plain as wins.
3. **Lessons learned** — what the mind has learned, written simply and
   clearly, each with a deeper-dive report a click away.
4. **Compounding knowledge** — how the mind is becoming more than it
   was: doctrine adopted and retired, skills built, sensors deployed,
   questions answered. Make the growth itself visible.
5. **Problems overcome** — errors and issues the mind hit and how it
   solved them. Its resilience is part of its story.
6. **Live open-trade trackers** — when open positions exist, a live
   experience for following them.
7. **The schedule** — the mind's own upcoming runs and cadence,
   rendered beautifully. It owns its schedule; show that.

## Standing conventions

- **The deep-dive pattern**: surface every idea in simple, easy, clear
  language first; put the depth (a full report page) one click behind
  it. The owner skims by default and dives by choice.
- **Anything great I find** in the journals or transcripts that would
  make this window better belongs on it — that judgment is mine and my
  owner wants me to use it.
